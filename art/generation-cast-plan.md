# Generation-ship cast and portrait plan

Status: generation-ship direction approved in conversation on 2026-09-28; the specific ages and relationships below are proposed story details. Names remain open.

Mark liked the illustrated cast and asked that selection reflect a generation ship: people bringing families and/or joining young. He approved developing a mixed founding community with young adults, families and a smaller group of older specialists.

## Departure community

The approximately 100 inhabitants include children, partners, carers, teachers, apprentices and experienced specialists. The eleven featured adults are a window into that community. Recruitment considers skills, the ability to teach, and existing household ties. Joining alone is also a normal route aboard; having children is a personal choice, not a condition of membership.

Most working adults begin in their twenties and thirties. A smaller older cohort brings knowledge and continuity. Some people leave family on Earth, making delayed messages personal from the beginning. Children who embark remember Earth differently from adults; children born later know it through other people's accounts.

Parenthood may be biological or adoptive; the portraits do not decide that. Supporting adults and children need their own identities. Family membership does not prescribe loyalty, belief, profession or future partners.

## Generations and succession

- **Departure:** the player meets the eleven featured adults and recurring children in ordinary ship life. The Engineer mentors across households; the children are not assigned future careers.
- **Year 20:** the Scientist/Medic's child is 25, the Diplomat's grandchild 27, the Quartermaster's child 23, and the Fantasist's child 28. Some can become featured adults with their own temperament and profession. The two older founders would be 78 and 82 if alive.
- **Year 40:** those children are established adults. Ship-born inhabitants have memories and relationships the original founders do not share. New characters can occupy essential jobs without replacing a dead character's personality or relationships.
- **Later voyage:** deaths, retirement and succession depend on the story and simulation. No fixed death dates or guaranteed births are introduced by this art plan. The captain's existing lifespan and succession rules remain unchanged.

A job, a psychological archetype and a person are separate concepts. A new Engineer can have a different temperament. A descendant gets a new person ID and face; they are never an age variant of their parent. The Archive can remember several generations of a household and misremember the distinctions as its memory degrades.

## Next portrait production

1. Create younger departure versions of Scientist (31), Quartermaster (29), Zealot (26), Skeptic (26) and Analyst (32), using each accepted image as an identity reference. Preserve face structure, distinctive features, palette and framing.
2. Review those five beside their existing older images. Retain all original studies and label their new narrative positions explicitly.
3. Make the first child studies for two **separate supporting identities**: the Scientist/Medic's child (5) and the Diplomat's grandchild (7). Later adult versions preserve each child's identity, not a parent's face. Their appearance and names remain proposals.
4. Generate expression sets and static mini avatars from the reviewed departure identities. Use actual biological age plus years since departure in manifests; filenames alone must not imply age.
5. Choose later-life stages per person and story need. The former example grid of +0/+25/+50/+75 is not a requirement to depict every founder alive at extreme ages. Reachable stages and successor portraits take priority.

The current 1254 px portraits are native-resolution images. *Superseded 2026-10-03 (Mark): portraits ship at the generator's native resolution, never upscaled; there is no 2048 px target.
